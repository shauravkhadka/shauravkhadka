## About

I'm an AI engineer and doctoral researcher at **Charles Darwin University** in Darwin, Australia. My interests sit at the intersection of human-centred AI, intelligent systems, and applied machine learning: how we build capable AI systems while preserving the information, judgment, and control people need to use them responsibly.

My technical background spans natural language processing, quantum machine learning, computer vision, and robotics. I approach projects as opportunities to connect research questions with working implementations and careful evaluation.

## Research interests

- **Human-centred AI:** meaningful human oversight, decision boundaries, transparency, and the role of human judgment in AI-supported workflows.
- **Machine learning and NLP:** sentiment analysis, topic discovery, semantic retrieval, and evaluation of language-based systems.
- **Quantum machine learning:** hybrid quantum–classical models, quantum circuit simulation, and non-Markovian dynamics.
- **Applied intelligent systems:** turning methods into usable tools, from data dashboards to perception and robotics prototypes.
